01

Ingest everything

Point BlueMetric at anything that emits: SDK, OpenTelemetry, Kafka, or plain logs. It reads the whole signal — and normalizes nothing. Raw stays raw; the models adapt to you, not the other way around.

02

Model every moving part

Each service, queue, and feature gets a live statistical twin. Baselines re-learn around seasonality, deploys, campaigns, and the chaos nobody planned for — continuously, in the background.

03

Forecast the drift

When a twin drifts, you get a forecast instead of a fire drill: what is about to happen, how confident the model is, and the exact window you have to act. Thresholds guess. Twins know.

04

Land it where work happens

Findings route to Slack, PagerDuty, or your ticketing — with the diff, the likely cause, and a one-click runbook. The on-call engineer opens one notification, not nine tabs.
